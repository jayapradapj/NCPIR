Founding Pillars

Founder Chairman

Nehru Group of Institutions was established in the year 1968 by Late PK. Das at Coimbatore in Tamil Nadu. His main objective was to provide quality professional education at an affordable cost. An educationist at heart, Das's vision was to ignite the spirit and nurture excellence among young minds. 50 years later even today, both his children are working towards this vision.

What we do?

Nehru Corporate Placement and Industry Relations [NCP & IR] – Is a vibrant, supportive and collaborative workplace with a great team that works hard and has fun doing meaningful work. The team immensely enjoys their part in contributing to student’s career of their choice.

In short, our goal is to achieve – right candidate for the right job, thus contributing and facilitating towards Molding a TRUE CITIZEN - a phrase which NGI strongly believes in.

As Career and Placement Specialists, our responsibilities are multifold; some of them being:

01

An interface between the institution and industry- with the primary objective of nurturing long-lasting and mutually beneficial relationship with institute and industry.

02

We provide counseling, training and support services to develop individual career plan, so that students perform better during placement drives.

03

Developing and providing industry linked internships, seminars, workshops, guest lecturers, industrial visits, etc.

04

Maintain an ever-growing database of corporate and industries- so as to identify varied job opportunities and to achieve win win combination.

05

Develop industry contact through established industrial committees and forms, by attending and presenting our strengths and strategies at various industrial events.

NEHRU CORPORATE PLACEMENT AND INDUSTRY RELATIONS [NCPIR]

As part of NGI mission to promote lifelong learning, NCPIR extends the resources of NGI through professional development and personal enrichment opportunities. Its wide area of skill sets offered addresses the need of the individual learners’ personal and professional growth.

The training programs are aimed at enhancing the employability skill set of the student, in which every graduate must hone their Leadership, Communication, Time Management, Problem Solving and Interpersonal skills. The programs offered to Graduates and Post Graduates are continuously monitored and revised to incorporate latest methodologies, technologies, theories and practices.

Although a strong background in traditional skills like mathematics and science will always have its place in academic and career worlds, today an increasing numbers of employers desire prospective employees with skills that enable individuals to adapt to changing circumstances and exhibit willingness to learn through experience.

At NCPIR, we take it upon ourselves to CREATE skilled, eligible and employable students. This is not achieved by NCPIR alone — every student also receives a strong academic foundation, so that they can COLLABORATE effectively using core skills in mathematics, science, and technology. Together, this builds a healthy BALANCE between learnt knowledge and acquired skill, which forms the basis of successful recruitment.

RIDDHI

Offer Letter Distribution Ceremony — Success Emulated

RIDDHI – The annual event at Nehru Group of Institutions – which is also the feather on the cap - marks the success of our students who have achieved placement in campus recruitment drives of various companies. The function of RIDDHI is to celebrate student's success; both the Placement & Training teams at NCPIR have systematically contributed with equal support from academics. Similarly, as a parent, it's a proud moment in seeing their son/daughter achieve' success in both academics' and successfully getting placed.

As a student, it is the outcome of hard work throughout and also gaining employability through the acquisition of necessary skills and attitude. Thus RIDDHI is a function wherein all stakeholders of NGI have everything to smile and celebrate. In this function, the employment offer letter for each candidate is distributed in the presence of their parents and college management.

Placement Process

Corporate Drive Onboarding

The Placement Team takes the initiative to explore new career avenues for graduates and post-graduates through visits to new and core companies and unexplored sectors. Campus recruitments are organized every year. The selection process generally starts with a Guest Lecture, pre-placement talk, written test, group discussion, and interview — company specific. Areas like Summer Internships, Industrial Visits and In-plant training are also offered.

1 📋
Nomination & Eligibility Screening

Initial processing of candidates based on prerequisite selection guidelines — CGPA, attendance & department approval.

2 🎓
Pre-Placement Training & Talk (JD Description)

Provides structural clarity regarding CTC, growth paths, and core job deliverables — bridging campus knowledge with corporate expectations.

3 🏫
Campus Placement Drive

Execution of written aptitude diagnostics, technical panel discussions, HR rounds, and final interviews — all on-campus.

4 👔
Corporate & Workplace Etiquette Training

Polishing soft-skills metrics to transition smoothly into standard business setups — grooming, communication, and work-culture alignment.

5 🎉
Offer Letter — RIDDHI 🌟

Formal allocation and delivery of corporate selection credentials and onboarding documents. Your Nehru degree takes off here.
